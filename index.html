Frequently asked questions.

01Which Katon 360 plan is right for our organisation?

Choose a starting plan based on your number of users, collaboration needs, organisational complexity, governance requirements and expected use of premium capabilities. Larger organisations can speak with the Katon team about their specific requirements.

02Can we scale our plan as our organisation grows?

The plan structure is designed around different team sizes and business needs. Contact the Katon team to discuss seat changes, upgrades and commercial arrangements.

03How can our IT team manage users and access?

Organisations should assess administration, identity management, user roles, permissions and access controls against their internal requirements. The Katon team can discuss the relevant capabilities for your deployment.

04How does Katon 360 approach security and privacy?

Security and privacy are important considerations across the ecosystem. Organisations with specific security, compliance, audit or governance requirements should discuss them with Katon before deployment.

05Can Katon 360 support multilingual teams?

Katon 360's multilingual collaboration approach is designed to support teams working across African and global language contexts. Contact the team for details about supported languages and translation capabilities.

06Can we evaluate Katon 360 before a wider rollout?

Contact the Katon team to discuss a product demonstration or evaluation and understand the product experience, deployment requirements and suitability for your organisation.

07How should we assess the total cost?

Consider user count, required capabilities, storage, AI usage, administration and support requirements. The Katon team can help clarify the proposed plan structure and commercial options.

08How can we discuss enterprise deployment requirements?

Contact the Katon team to discuss organisational needs, deployment requirements, governance considerations and appropriate commercial arrangements.
